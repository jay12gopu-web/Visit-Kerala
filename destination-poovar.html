South Kerala Estuary

Poovar

Mangrove-lined waterways, a shifting sandbar and coastal village life where the Neyyar River meets the Arabian Sea.

Why Poovar

A river, backwater and beach in one landscape.

Poovar sits near Kerala's southern tip, around 35 kilometres from Thiruvananthapuram. Boat journeys move through the Neyyar estuary past mangroves, fishing boats and coconut-lined banks towards a golden sandbar. It works best as a daylight excursion from Kovalam or as a quiet overnight extension.

What To Experience

Between River And Sea

On The Water

Neyyar Estuary Cruise

Take a locally operated boat through the river mouth and narrow waterways, with life jackets and conditions confirmed before departure.

Nature

Mangroves & Birdlife

Watch kingfishers, egrets and waterside vegetation quietly from the boat without entering fragile mangrove areas.

Coast

Golden Sandbar

See the seasonal strip of sand between the estuary and sea. Landing and swimming depend on tides, currents and local safety advice.

The Story

A southern village shaped by water.

Poovar's identity comes from the Neyyar River, the Arabian Sea and the fishing communities between them. The estuary changes with tides and monsoon flow, so every visit can look slightly different. Respect working boats, homes and fishing activity; this is a lived-in coastal landscape rather than a staged attraction.

Taste The South

Fresh, coconut-rich coastal food.

  • Kerala fish curry with rice
  • Appam with vegetable or fish stew
  • Tapioca with spicy fish curry
  • Banana and coconut snacks
Build It In

Add Poovar to a southern coast stay.

Base yourself in Kovalam or Thiruvananthapuram and reserve a half or full day for Poovar. Start early, confirm tide and weather conditions with the operator, and avoid combining the cruise with a long same-day transfer.

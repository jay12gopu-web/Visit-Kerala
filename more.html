About Us

About Visit Kerala

Visit Kerala is a student-created tourism planning project for educational and exhibition purposes. It is inspired by publicly available Kerala Tourism information and helps visitors understand routes, places, travel styles and practical planning choices.

  • Useful for families, couples, solo travellers, friends, student groups and senior travellers.
  • Built to support exploration and planning, not to act as a booking website.
  • Designed with popular highlights and quieter Kerala experiences side by side.
